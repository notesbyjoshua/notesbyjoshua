This domain focuses on equivalent expressions and nonlinear equations, systems, and functions.

## Recognizing the Question Type

| Question type | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Equivalent expressions | Factoring, expanding, combining fractions, or rewriting an expression without changing its value |
| Nonlinear equation in one variable | Quadratic, exponential, radical, rational, or absolute-value expressions with one unknown |
| System of equations | Two relationships, with at least one nonlinear, that must be satisfied together |
| Nonlinear function | A curved graph or a rule involving powers, roots, exponentials, or rational expressions |

:::tip
First decide whether the task asks you to rewrite an expression, solve for an input, solve a system, or interpret a function. Similar-looking algebra can require different goals.
:::
