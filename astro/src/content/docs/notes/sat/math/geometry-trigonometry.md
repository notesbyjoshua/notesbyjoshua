This domain focuses on geometric measurement, relationships between lines and angles, triangles, trigonometry, and circles.

## Recognizing the Question Type

| Question type | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| Area and volume | A two-dimensional region, three-dimensional solid, surface area, or volume |
| Lines, angles, and triangles | Parallel lines, angle relationships, similarity, congruence, or triangle properties |
| Right triangles and trigonometry | A right angle, the Pythagorean theorem, special triangles, or sine, cosine, and tangent |
| Circles | A radius, diameter, circumference, area, arc, sector, tangent, or equation of a circle |

:::tip
Sketch and label the figure, even when a diagram is provided. Mark the requested quantity and copy only the measurements and relationships that matter before selecting a formula.
:::
