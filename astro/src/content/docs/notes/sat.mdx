## Reading and Writing

Reading and Writing prep will cover:

- understanding information and ideas in passages
- analyzing the purpose and structure of a text
- revising sentences for clarity and organization
- using standard English conventions
- choosing precise words and transitions

## How to Use This Section

Start by identifying the topics that cost you the most points. Review those skills, practice them without a time limit, and then add timed sets once your method is consistent.

:::strategy{title="A simple SAT study cycle"}
1. Take a practice set and record every missed or guessed question.
2. Sort each question by topic and by the reason you missed it.
3. Review the weakest skill and practice similar questions.
4. Retake missed questions without looking at the solution.
5. Use a timed mixed set to check whether the improvement carries over.
:::
